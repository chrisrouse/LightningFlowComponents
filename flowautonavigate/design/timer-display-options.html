Timer display options

Three treatments of one countdown, a 95-second demo. Each turns to its warning state in the last 10 seconds.

Running

Ring

1:35

A thin arc around the number. Shows how much is left without competing with the screen's content.

Bar

Advances in 1:35

Full width, so it reads across a room. The dark mark on the track is where the warning starts.

Pill

1:35

The smallest footprint, for a header or beside other content. The warning needs the tint and the pulse together to stay readable.
